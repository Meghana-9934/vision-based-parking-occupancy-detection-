About ParkDetect

Revolutionizing parking management with cutting-edge AI technology

Our Story

ParkDetect was born from a simple observation: finding parking in urban areas is a frustrating and time-consuming experience. Our team of AI researchers and urban planners came together to develop a solution that would make parking more efficient for everyone.

Since our inception in 2020, we've grown from a small startup to a leading provider of smart parking solutions, with implementations in over 50 cities worldwide. Our technology has helped millions of drivers find parking faster and enabled parking operators to optimize their spaces.

Today, we continue to innovate and improve our system, incorporating the latest advances in computer vision and machine learning to provide the most accurate and reliable parking detection solution on the market.

Our Technology

Advanced Neural Networks

Our system uses state-of-the-art convolutional neural networks (CNNs) specifically trained for parking space detection. These models can accurately identify parking spaces and determine their occupancy status even in challenging lighting and weather conditions.

Real-Time Video Processing

ParkDetect processes video feeds in real-time, analyzing frames every few seconds to provide up-to-date information about parking availability. Our optimized algorithms ensure minimal latency while maintaining high accuracy.

Cloud-Based Infrastructure

Our cloud-based architecture ensures scalability and reliability, allowing the system to handle multiple parking facilities simultaneously. This also means easier maintenance and updates without disrupting service.

Privacy-First Design

We prioritize privacy in our system design. ParkDetect only analyzes parking spaces and does not identify or store personal information about vehicles or individuals. All data is anonymized and processed securely.
